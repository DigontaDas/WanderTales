# WanderTales
Purpose: Create a platform where travelers can share their journey experiences, give travel tips and review services like hotels or tour companies.

Target User: Travelers, Backpackers, Tourists and Travel bloggers.
References:
OpenWeatherAPI:  https://openweathermap.org/ 
MERN Stack Documentation:
MongoDB
Express.js
React.js
Node.js
Functional Requirements:
Rate and check hotels & restaurants with reviews
Read others blogs and stories also share own stories
Check destinations with additional information
Upload, organize, and share travel photos
Real-time weather data for destinations
Search by destination, user, tags, or content
Users can like & comment on stories 
Displays the user's stories, reviews, follower count, and bio
Admins can manage users, remove content, or moderate reported items
Suggest travel blogs and reviews based on the user’s current or selected location
